About BGSA Learn about our organization & leadership

The Bangladesh Graduate Student Association (BGSA) was established with the motivation to foster a stronger sense of community and enhance connectivity among members. One of the association’s primary goals has been to support graduate students and their families as they settle in and navigate their academic journey abroad.

In 2022, a small group of four students stepped forward, with support from the broader community, to form the first leadership committee. They organized several meet-and-greet events featuring cultural activities and Bengali cuisine. From this modest beginning, subsequent committees have grown in size and expanded BGSA’s functions, activities, and impact both within the organization and across the community.

Today, BGSA serves as a strong anchor connecting graduate students, post-doctoral researchers, Bengali faculty members, and even student groups from other universities. With increasingly effective leadership and continued community support, BGSA aims to remain at the forefront of improving the experience of every graduate student and their family as they navigate their journey at OSU.
