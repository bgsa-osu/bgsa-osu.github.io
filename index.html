About BGSA Learn about our organization & leadership

The Bangladesh Graduate Student Association (BGSA) at The Ohio State University is a vibrant and inclusive community dedicated to supporting Bangladeshi graduate students throughout their academic journey. As the officially recognized organization representing Bangladeshi graduate scholars at OSU, BGSA strives to create an environment where students feel connected, empowered, and proud of their heritage. Rooted in cultural identity and community spirit, the organization serves as both a home and a hub; bringing students together to celebrate shared traditions, build friendships, and navigate graduate life with confidence and support. BGSA is committed to meaningful engagement, cultural exchange, and positive social impact within the broader Buckeye community. Through events, initiatives, and collaborative projects, the association works to foster understanding of Bangladeshi culture, strengthen ties between international and domestic students, and create opportunities for leadership, service, and personal growth. In addition to cultural and social programming, BGSA emphasizes student wellbeing and academic success. The organization offers a supportive network for new and returning students, helping them transition to life at OSU, build peer connections, and access essential university resources. BGSA operates with a strong focus on inclusivity, ensuring that all students; regardless of background—feel welcome and valued. Led by a dedicated Executive Committee and guided by a faculty Advisor, BGSA upholds the highest standards of professionalism, equity, and compliance with university regulations. The association stands firmly against discrimination, harassment, and hazing, embracing a culture of respect, dignity, and community care. Above all, BGSA exists to celebrate the rich identity of the Bangladeshi community at Ohio State, provide a platform for student voices, and cultivate a sense of belonging that lasts far beyond graduation. Through unity, collaboration, and cultural pride, BGSA continues to enrich the OSU experience for current and future generations of students. ￼
