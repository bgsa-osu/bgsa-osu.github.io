About BGSA Learn about our organization & leadership

The Bangladeshi Graduate Student Association (BGSA) at The Ohio State University is a student-led organization committed to fostering community, collaboration, and cultural exchange among Bangladeshi graduate students, alumni, and researchers. BGSA organizes academic, social, and cultural events that strengthen connections within the OSU community while providing mentorship and support for new students.

Freshers' Reception 2025

As many of you know, our new Executive Committee officially took responsibility on October 15, and since then we’ve been actively engaged in completing the administrative and registration procedures for our organization. We deeply appreciate your patience and continued support during this transition period. We’re excited to officially announce that our very first event, Freshers’ Reception 2025 is in the works, a special event to welcome our new Bangladeshi graduate students and bring our community together! We’re planning to host this event on Sunday, November 30, 2025. Please note the date is tentative and will be confirmed soon once logistics are finalized. Stay tuned for updates, and thank you once again to everyone contributing their time and ideas to make this event a success.
